    Rules of engagement

    • Deadly force is authorized only against armed, non-compliant suspects. Shooting unarmed or surrendered people is penalized.
    • Yell (F) repeatedly — every warning raises pressure. Flashbangs, stingers, gas, pepperball, beanbags and tasers make compliance far more likely.
    • Compliant people kneel with hands up: restrain them. Some suspects fake surrender — restrain fast and keep your sights on them.
    • Report every downed or dead person to TOC, and collect dropped weapons and evidence.
    • Mirror under doors to see who is inside and spot traps. Locked doors can be picked, kicked, rammed, shot open with a shotgun or blown with C2.
    • Hostage-takers hide behind civilians — use less-lethal or wait for a clean shot.
